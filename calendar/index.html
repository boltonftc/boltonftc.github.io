    Subscribe

    Put it on your calendar

    Subscribing (instead of importing) means cancellations and new events update on their own. Calendar apps usually refresh a subscribed feed every few hours.

    1. GGoogle Calendar Opens Google Calendar and asks to add the feed. On a phone, do this once from a computer and it syncs everywhere.
    2. AApple Calendar · iPhone · Mac Opens the Calendar app's subscribe dialog. Choose "Subscribe" and pick an alert if you want reminders.
    3. OOutlook Adds the feed to Outlook on the web. Desktop Outlook: Add calendar → From internet, then paste the feed link below.
    4. ↓Download .ics A one-time snapshot for any other app. It will not update when the schedule changes, so subscribing is better.
