Parallel Plate CAD & Design Session

Keep refining the parallel plate bot CAD. Fixes and additions for this session:

  • Hole-to-edge spacing should be 0.4 so the dual blocks mount correctly.
  • All 4 mm holes need tolerance — size them a touch over (≈4.1 mm) so bolts pass through cleanly instead of being dead-on 4 mm.
  • Add a spot for the intake motor(s) — leave room and mounting for where the intake will drive from.

Review Team 15996 Brickset Bot CAD — Decode Turret Shooter

Study Team 15996's Brickset Bot turret shooter from the Decode season. What can we learn from it? Look at how the turret rotates, how the shooter mounts, and any ideas we can borrow for our own shooter.
