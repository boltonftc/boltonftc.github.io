📅 October 28, 2026 🏷️ Training, Autos & Robot Work

Tonight we split our time between finishing the Java training (with mentor sign-off for the engineering portfolio), a new simulator lesson that adds shooting to autonomous, and hands-on work on both robots: the Gurt turret, pollen removal on Gurt and Gabe, and driver practice.

Programming

💻 Training & autonomous

🎓

Everyone: complete all Java training lessons — with mentor sign-off

Every student works toward completing all of the lessons in the Impulse 3D Simulator. When you pass a lesson's quiz, show a mentor so they can sign it off. The signed sheet goes into our engineering portfolio, so it's real evidence of what our team learned.

🌼

New lesson: Actions in Autonomous — Make It Shoot

There's a new last lesson in the simulator. It explains how an OpMode really runs — the run loop, robot.update(), why sleep() freezes the robot, for loops and opModeIsActive() — and how to mark a shooting spot in Path Capture by pressing X.

Pass its quiz to unlock the code that makes a captured auto spin up the flywheel and shoot. Then add it to your auto and test it in the simulator.
