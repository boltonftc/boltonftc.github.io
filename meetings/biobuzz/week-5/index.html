Week 5 — Teardown, Sort & Rebuild

🔧

Activity 1 — Disassemble 36564 & Sort Parts

Take apart the 36564 robot and sort the parts into their bins.

Keep the motor + quad block + gear assembled as one unit — we can reuse that assembly, so don't break it down.

🧰

Activity 2 — Fully Disassemble 36563 & Sort Parts

Take the 36563 robot all the way apart and sort everything.

  • Bolts go into a misc bag; all other parts get sorted.
  • Any part with multiple components (like a two-part clamp held together with bolts) should be re-assembled with its pieces before it goes in a bin, so we don't lose the little pieces.
  • We're generally combining parts and tools together — we'll see if we have enough.
🤖
