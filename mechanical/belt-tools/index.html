New to timing belts?

A timing belt is a toothed loop that links two toothed pulleys so they turn together without slipping. The ratio of their teeth sets the trade between speed and torque: a 24T pulley driving a 48T pulley is a 2:1 reduction, half the speed and twice the torque.

For the parts themselves, see Robot Hardware.

The catch

Belts only come in certain lengths, so the center-to-center distance between the shafts has to match a belt that exists. That's the problem these tools solve. If you're between two sizes, round up to the slightly looser belt and take up the slack with a tensioner. Never round down.
