How it works

  • Do the lessons in order.Each one builds on the skills from the last.
  • Try each exercise before opening its walkthrough.The videos are there for when you're really stuck, not to copy along.
  • Save everything in the team training folder.Lesson 01 shows you where it is.

You'll need

  • A free Onshape accountUse a student/education account so it stays free.
  • A computer with a mouseA trackpad works, but a three-button mouse makes 3D much easier.
  • The FTC parts libraryFrom Lesson 05 on. Setup steps are in that lesson.
