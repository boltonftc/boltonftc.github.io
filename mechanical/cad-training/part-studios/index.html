A Part Studio is Onshape's 3D modeling workspace, and it's where you'll make every part you ever design. You draw sketches, then use features like Extrude, Revolve and Fillet to turn them into solid geometry.

Introduction to Part Studios

Watch every lesson, pass the knowledge checks, then build all five exercises.

Open the course →
Key idea

Every feature you add is recorded, in order, in the Feature List on the left. You can go back and edit any step and everything after it rebuilds. No redrawing needed.

When it looks wrong

Check the Feature List first. Onshape flags problems right on the feature that broke: click the red warning to see why. Most of the time it's a missing dimension or a sketch that isn't fully closed.
