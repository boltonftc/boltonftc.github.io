Many competitive FTC teams use parallel plate drivetrains, and they make a solid base for designing your own robot. You'll create new parts, modify existing ones, and put together a much bigger assembly than before. It won't go perfectly the first time. Getting stuck and working your way out is the point of the exercise.

Before you start: the FTC parts library

You need standard FTC hardware (motors, wheels, bearings, channel) to drop into your assembly. There are two ways to get it:

  1. FTC Insert Tool (easiest). Add it from the Onshape App Store: FTC Insert Tool.
  2. Request library access. Follow the steps at ftconshape.com and email for access to the full FTC parts library.

The tutorial

  1. Make a new Onshape document in your training folder. See Lesson 01 if you can't find it.
  2. Watch one section of the video, switch to Onshape and build that part, then come back for the next section. Don't watch the whole thing first.
  3. Keep going until the whole drivetrain is built.
