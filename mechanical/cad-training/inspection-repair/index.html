Key idea

A missing reference happens when a feature was built on geometry that's gone. For example, you delete a sketch that an Extrude depended on. Onshape marks it in red in the Feature List, and the repair tools let you point the feature at new geometry instead of rebuilding it.

Why bother now?

These tools make the most sense once you've broken a few models for real. Even if nothing breaks during the exercises, pay attention. Fixing a broken model quickly is one of the skills that separates beginners from confident designers.
