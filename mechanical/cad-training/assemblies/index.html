An Assembly is the workspace where separate parts come together: every motor, bracket, shaft and gear placed where it belongs relative to everything else. The links between parts are called mates.

Key idea

A mate says how two parts connect and how they can move. A fastened mate locks them together, like a bolt. A revolute mate lets one spin around an axis, like a hinge or an axle. Picking the right mate is what makes a virtual assembly behave like the real thing.

Part flew off somewhere odd?

Onshape highlights mate connectors as you hover. If a part jumps to a strange place, you probably picked a face instead of the axis you meant. Press Ctrl+Z and pick again.
