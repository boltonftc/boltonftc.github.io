GT2 belt tools

Timing belts only come in certain lengths, so the gap between your shafts can't be just anything. Tell the calculator what you already know and it finds the pulleys, the exact goBILDA belt, and where to put the shafts.

  • Design itTarget ratio + rough spacing → ranked pulley and belt combinations
  • Find a beltPulleys + spacing → nearest stock belts and the shaft move for each
  • Place the shaftsPulleys + the belt you have → exact center-to-center distance
