Driver control map for our BIOBUZZ robot — single driver, gamepad 1. Every binding below is read straight from the finished MecanumDrive TeleOp. Buttons not listed do nothing.

Driving

  • Left stick — drive (↕) & strafe (↔), field-centric
  • Left trigger — turn left (spin CCW)
  • Right trigger — turn right (spin CW)
  • Right bumper — reset heading (re-zero field-centric)

Shooter

  • Y — flywheel on/off (toggle)
  • Right stick ↕ — trim flywheel speed (up = stronger)
  • Right stick ↔ — aim the hood
  • X — feed a piece into the shooter (hold)

Intake

  • A — intake IN (press to toggle)
  • B — intake REVERSE (press to toggle)

Unused

  • Left bumper, D-pad
  • Stick clicks (L3 / R3)
  • View, Menu, Guide
Toggle = one press flips it on or off (not held). Hold = active only while the button is down. All controls are on gamepad 1 (one driver).
