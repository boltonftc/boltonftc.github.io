Already know your two pulley sizes and the center-to-center distance between the shafts? Enter them below and this finds the goBILDA® belts that fit closest — and how far you'd nudge a shaft to make each one perfect.

How it works. From your two pulleys and center distance it computes the ideal belt length, then checks every goBILDA belt: for each one it back-solves the exact center distance that belt needs, and shows the small shaft move (ΔC) to reach it. Rule of thumb — round up to the next belt (slightly loose, take up the slack with a tensioner/idler); never round down or the belt rides too tight.
