How it works. For every pair of pulleys in your tooth range whose ratio is within tolerance, the Wizard computes the exact belt length needed at your target spacing, then matches that against real goBILDA belts. Each real belt fixes the geometry, so it back-solves the exact center-to-center distance that belt requires and keeps the ones landing within your center tolerance. Results are ranked by how close the center distance lands to your target (then by ratio). Click any row to preview it in the diagram above.
