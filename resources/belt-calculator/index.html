New to timing belts? A timing belt is a toothed loop that links two toothed pulleys so they turn together without slipping. The ratio of teeth sets the speed/torque trade (a 24T pulley driving a 48T is a 2:1 reduction). The center-to-center distance is how far apart the two shafts sit — and it has to match a belt length that actually exists, which is what these tools solve for. All belts here are goBILDA® 2 mm GT2 continuous loops; verify current stock & pricing on goBILDA before ordering.
