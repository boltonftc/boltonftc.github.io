--- layout: page title: "Find Center-to-Center" ---

Already have a belt in hand? Pick your two pulleys and the exact goBILDA® belt, and this gives the center-to-center distance to place your two shafts so the belt runs at the right tension.

How it works. A belt of a fixed length wrapping two known pulleys can only sit at one center-to-center distance — this solves that geometry exactly and reports it, along with each pulley's pitch diameter and the wrap angle on the smaller pulley (keep it above ~120° so teeth don't skip under load). If the belt is too short to reach around both pulleys, it says so — pick a longer one.
